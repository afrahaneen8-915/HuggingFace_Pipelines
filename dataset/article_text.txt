Each one shared a common belief for the year ahead: the pace of innovation won’t slow down in 2026. “It’s such a crazy time,” Peter Staar, a Principal Research Staff Member at the IBM Research Zurich Laboratory, told IBM Think in an interview. “And it’s only accelerating.” New agentic capabilities will give way to new possibilities for businesses and individuals alike. “I really see the parallels of music production à la Rick Rubin style with AI creation,” IBM’s Distinguished Engineer Chris Hay told IBM Think. “I don’t limit it to coding. At the same time, trust and security will become key priorities as many enterprises sharpen their focus on AI sovereignty.
